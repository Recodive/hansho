This is where you look after who receives messages from {{.Hub}}. Choose what you want to do.

  • Pair a personMake a pair code. The person scans it with the Hansho app to start receiving messages.
  • PeopleSee who is paired and on which devices. Remove someone who should stop receiving messages.
  • ChannelsMake and change channels: how loud their messages may get, and who may receive them.
  • GroupsName a list of people, such as your on-call team, to let a channel include or leave out all of them at once.
  • Send a test messageSend a message to one person to check that a channel reaches them.
  • API keysKeys let your own software send messages through this hub. Make a new one or switch one off.
